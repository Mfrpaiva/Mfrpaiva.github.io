Extension Philosophy

For me, extension is not an accessory to research — it is its social dimension.

Whether through scientific videos, institutional educational research, sustainability outreach, or international engagement, I seek to translate knowledge into shared understanding.

If catalysis transforms molecules, extension transforms perspectives.

And both are necessary to build a more sustainable and scientifically informed society.
