<strong style="color: #4a9fd8;">Expanding Science Beyond the Laboratory</strong>

For me, extension is not an accessory to research — it is its social dimension. Whether through scientific videos, institutional educational research, sustainability outreach, or international engagement, I seek to translate knowledge into shared understanding. If catalysis transforms molecules, extension transforms perspectives. And both are necessary to build a more sustainable and scientifically informed society.<br>

My work is closely linked to outreach and science communication initiatives aimed at popularizing catalysis and sustainable chemistry. One of these initiatives resulted in the award-winning science communication video:
🎥<a href="https://youtu.be/psEIIGJiRBM?si=eYuQBZ_BH9v4Uu_M" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"> <span style="color: #4a9fd8;">“From a Moment Available, a Useful Force”</span> </a> (Video Destaque Award – Brazilian Catalysis Society, SBCat, 2020). The recognition by SBCat reflects my commitment to making chemistry visible, understandable, and socially relevant.<br>

<strong style="color: #4a9fd8;">Educational Research & Student Retention><br>
During my undergraduate years at the University of Brasília (UnB), I conceived and led an extension project within the PET Program focused on student retention in Chemistry programs. 🔎I developed a structured methodology to:<br>
-Analyze institutional data on dropout rates;<br>
-Identify underlying causes of student attrition;<br>
-Propose institutional-level reflections and improvements.<br>

This initiative culminated in presentations delivered to the entire Institute of Chemistry community during the VII Chemistry Week at UnB: Paiva, M. F. *Dropout in Undergraduate Chemistry Programs.* VII Chemistry Week – University of Brasília, **2014**.<br>

This project was not merely diagnostic; it fostered institutional dialogue and raised awareness about academic permanence, pedagogical practices, and student support structures. It marked an early expression of my commitment to strengthening academic formation systems from within.<br>

<strong style="color: #4a9fd8;">Sustainability Outreach – GCVBio/UFF (2024 – Present)><br>
Currently, as part of the GCVBio-UFF team, I contribute to an extension project focused on:<br>
-🍃Dissemination of sustainability, circular economy, and green chemistry concepts to the community of Niterói (RJ, Brazil).<br>

This initiative promotes:<br>
-Environmental education for high school students;<br>
-Training for undergraduate chemistry students;<br>
-Engagement with the broader community;<br>
-Alignment with the Brazilian National Common Curricular Base (BNCC);<br>
-Contribution to the UN Sustainable Development Goals (Agenda 2030).<br>

By integrating research, education, and public engagement, this project strengthens the social dimension of chemistry and reinforces the role of universities as transformative agents.<br>

<strong style="color: #4a9fd8;">International Outreach & Interdisciplinary Engagement – Université de Lille><br>
During my PhD at Université de Lille, I actively participated in the interdisciplinary program: **Science for a Changing Planet (SCP)** 🌎👨‍🔬<br>

This initiative integrated environmental sciences, climate change research, and sustainability, preparing researchers to address complex global challenges. Within this framework, I:<br>
-Mentored incoming Master’s students in Chemistry;<br>
-Presented my doctoral research at scientific fairs and outreach events;<br>
-Engaged in interdisciplinary dialogue on climate-oriented research.<br>

This experience reinforced my conviction that scientific knowledge must circulate beyond specialized audiences, connecting research with global environmental challenges.
