In a virtual ceremony held on September 10, 2020, the Brazilian Catalysis Society (SBCat), through its board of directors, announced that student Mateus Freitas Paiva, enrolled in the Academic Master’s Program in Chemistry at the University of Brasília (PPGQ-UnB) under the supervision of Professor Dr. José Alves Dias, was awarded the 2020 Highlight Video in Catalysis Prize.

With the video entitled “From an Available Moment, a Useful Force,” Mateus had his work recognized for its relevance, technical quality, peer-review evaluation, and public outreach impact. The Highlight Video in Catalysis Prize was established to strengthen science communication initiatives in the field of Catalysis.

The student’s video can be viewed at: https://youtu.be/psEIIGJiRBM

The virtual ceremony in which the result was announced is available at: https://youtu.be/8aVPff33tF8
