Research conducted at the Instituto Nacional de Tecnologia receives award from the Royal Society of Chemistry

The Instituto Nacional de Tecnologia (INT) received an award during the 6th International Congress on Catalysis for Biorefineries (CATBIOR 2026), held in Dalian, China, from April 25 to 29. The event is one of the most important international conferences in the field of catalysis applied to biomass valorization and the development of sustainable biorefineries.

Representing INT, chemist Mateus Freitas Paiva, researcher of the Programa Conhecimento Brasil from Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico (CNPq), presented the work entitled “Catalytic approaches for biomass fractionation in molten salt hydrates and lignin valorization”. The research aims to develop a new type of pretreatment capable of separating biomass into its main fractions while improving the valorization of each component.

The work received the Poster Prize Award, granted by the Royal Society of Chemistry (RSC) through the journal Green Chemistry, one of the most prestigious journals in the field of chemistry.

This international recognition reinforces the relevance of the research developed at INT and its contribution to advancing more sustainable solutions.
